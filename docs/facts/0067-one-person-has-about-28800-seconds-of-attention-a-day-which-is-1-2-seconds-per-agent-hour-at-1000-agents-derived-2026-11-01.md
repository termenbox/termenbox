# One person has about 28,800 seconds of attention a day, which is 1.2 seconds per agent-hour at 1,000 agents and 0.12 at 10,000

Derived arithmetic, not a measurement. The 8-hour day is an assumption; change it and the rows scale.

| Agents running around the clock | Agent-hours a day | Human seconds per agent-hour (28,800 s a day) |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 2,400 | 12 |
| 1,000 | 24,000 | 1.2 |
| 10,000 | 240,000 | 0.12 |

What it means here: below about one second per agent-hour a person cannot decide per instance; they decide per class, per contract and per error budget (decisions 0165, 0168, 0172). Agents that work only part of the day raise the budget in proportion. Attention leverage (R85) replaces the assumption with the owner's measured number.
