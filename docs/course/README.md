# Start the course

You are at your desk and you have just handed a long job to a coding agent. It will take an hour. You would rather not watch it, and you would rather not be tied to this chair either. So you leave. On the train an hour later your phone buzzes: the agent has a question. You answer it with your thumb, and the work carries on.

This course takes you from nothing to exactly that. Six short chapters, one short video each. Do them in order; each ends with something you can see on your screen.

| # | Chapter | You end with |
|---|---------|--------------|
| 1 | [Install](01-install.md) | A server running on your machine, showing a QR code. |
| 2 | [Connect your phone](02-connect-your-phone.md) | Your phone listing the server as connected. |
| 3 | [First workspace](03-first-workspace.md) | A folder and an agent, open on your phone. |
| 4 | [Talk to your agent](04-talk-to-your-agent.md) | A prompt sent, and the agent's answer on screen. |
| 5 | [Answer from the train](05-answer-from-the-train.md) | A waiting agent answered with one tap. |
| 6 | [Come back later](06-come-back-later.md) | The same screen, right where you left it. |

The Master part follows once these six have been followed by someone new to Termenbox: Terminal and Agent view, searching the scrollback, the key row, notifications in depth, several servers, and "it won't connect".
