Goal: the documentation videos and stills come from one recorder, are reviewed by the owner on a phone from a preview page, are sent back with `/regen` or approved by a tick, are stored on the orphan `docs-media` branch with an audit manifest, reach the site through `deploy-site`, and are embedded in the README and guide pages by markers; stale videos are detected; the CLI reference is generated and links, anchors and guide-versus-code drift are checked in CI.
