## Context

## Do

Work the plan: the `SessionLog` port and the Claude and Codex adapters, the bounded scan with its benchmark, the `search` and `window` protocol messages, the Search sheet, ACP scroll-to-turn through a history block in `AcpView`, tap to switch or resume, and the e2e.

## Done when

On a server with a Claude and a Codex session in open and dormant workspaces, a phrase typed in the Search sheet lists scrubbed hits from both; tapping a hit in an ACP workspace scrolls to and highlights the turn, tapping one in a terminal tab switches to it, and tapping one in a dormant workspace offers Resume. The query appears in no log line.
