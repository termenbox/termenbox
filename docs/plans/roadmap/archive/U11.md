## Notes

**Result 2026-10-24 (PR #291):** the ladder runs on the server's push path, one task per Attention item: a question (`needs_input`) is pushed after the grace, then after the user-set wait (default 10 minutes) times a multiplier up to a cap, and stops when the item leaves Attention (an answer anywhere). Quiet hours and a muted agent skip a push, the first included. Settings travel as `push_settings_get` and `push_settings_set` over the authenticated socket and are kept in `push_settings.json`; quiet hours are the owner's local time because the browser sends its UTC offset. The push body carries a deep link `/#/home?focus=<key>`; Home shows that session's decision card first. Server tests (`cargo test -p termenbox-server push`) prove the timing (`next_wait`), that an answer stops a ladder without stopping another, that quiet hours and mutes silence pushes, and that no marker text reaches the push; `e2e/push-link.mjs` proves cold start and open-app deep links and the settings form.

Not done or not proved: the client has no app lock, so "behind the app lock" has nothing to attach to yet. The service worker's `notificationclick` (navigate or open the link) is covered by neither test; U16 checks it on iPhone and Android.
