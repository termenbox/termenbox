## Why

Menus, paths, keyboard, shortcuts and navigation are judged by hand and by the owner's iPhone, and nothing runs the UX checks on every UI change or in WebKit. The owner also wants short videos of specific features for users.

## Context

`docs/plans/ux-assessment-harness.md` is the full plan, with cold-start steps 1.1 to 7.2. Decision 0135, guardrail `ux-hard-rules-block-the-pr-the-rest-is-advisory`, fact 0056 and the procedures `improve-the-ux-from-ci` and `render-a-feature-video` define the design. Builds on the R36 launcher `e2e/browser.mjs` (done) and adds device profiles to it. Real-device passes (R03, U16) stay a gate; this job is emulated only.

## Do

Work the plan: device profiles on the R36 launcher, scenario runner, inventory and rubric, phone, desktop and tablet scenarios, metrics, base-versus-head comparison, the `ux.yaml` workflow, the video renderer and dispatch, calibration, one full improvement pass.

## Done when

A UI pull request runs the UX job on Chromium and WebKit, comments a base-versus-head summary, and fails only on the seven hard rules; a dispatch renders a captioned MP4 and GIF for a scenario; three `develop` runs agree; one improvement pull request made through the procedure shows a better number.

## Check

**Class:** implement

`gh run list --workflow ux.yaml --limit 1 --json conclusion -q '.[0].conclusion'` prints `success` on `develop`, and `node packages/client/e2e/ux/flake-check.mjs --runs 3` reports no hard-rule difference.

## Notes

Tablet and landscape are assessed only; tablet layouts stay deferred until the real-device pass. No model call and no secret in CI. Publishing videos to the site is out of scope.
