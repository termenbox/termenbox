## Why

The README and the six guide pages describe behaviour and carry no video, no order and no first step, so a newcomer does not know what to do after the QR. The first rendered video also shows that flat random delays read as a fast robot.

## Context

`docs/plans/user-course.md` is the full plan with cold-start steps. Decisions 0160 (a guided course in `docs/course`, Start before Master) and 0161 (rhythm model, still poster, steps from the case, replayed installer; amends 0158), fact 0068, the guardrails `course-chapters-keep-the-fixed-anatomy-and-generated-steps` and `a-course-part-is-done-only-after-a-recorded-cold-read`, and the procedures `write-a-course-chapter`, `run-a-cold-read-test` and `regenerate-the-user-course` define the design. It builds on the media pipeline of R79 (decision 0158), which is on `develop`.

## Do

Work the plan: the rhythm model; steps declared by each case and generated into the chapters; a still poster instead of a GIF; six recorded cases (install by replaying the real installer, connect, first workspace, talk to your agent, answer from the train, come back later); the course index and six chapters; the course check; the README entry; a render, review and pick; a cold read on a real phone; then plan Master.

A newcomer opens the README, follows the course to a working phone, and watches six videos that move at a human pace; every chapter keeps its anatomy and generated steps in CI; a person new to Termenbox has followed it on a real phone and every stall they hit is fixed or filed.
