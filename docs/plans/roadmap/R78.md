## Why

Four dialog videos render in Actions but end in an expiring artifact. Nobody can review them on a phone, send one back, publish it, or learn that a UI change made it stale, and the README, six guide pages and the site carry no media and have almost no machine checks. A second recorder was started on a side branch and R73 would add a third.

## Context

`docs/plans/docs-media-and-checks.md` is the full plan with cold-start steps. Decisions 0149 (media pipeline, amends 0135) and 0150 (docs checks), facts 0064 and 0065, guardrails `media-commands-come-only-from-people-with-write-access` and `nothing-is-published-without-a-matching-approval-record`, and the procedures `review-and-publish-docs-media` and `render-a-feature-video` define the design. `e2e/video/` becomes the one recorder; R73 adopts it for its video renderer.
