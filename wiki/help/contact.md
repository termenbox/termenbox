# Contact

Termenbox is built and run by one person. Pick the route that fits; it gets the question to the right place the first time. Back to the [README](../../README.md). Related: [Questions](./faq.md), [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md).

## A question or a bug

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/termenbox/termenbox/discussions) under Q&A, so the answer helps the next person. For a bug, [open an issue](https://github.com/termenbox/termenbox/issues/new/choose) with your version and operating system. Do not paste passwords, tokens, pairing links or one-time secrets.

## A security problem

Email **security@termenbox.dev**. Do not open a public issue. The [security policy](../security/reporting.md) says what to include and what to expect.

## Partnerships, licensing and pilots

Email **hello@termenbox.dev**. This address is not for support; support questions get a pointer to Discussions. A short note about who you are and what you want to do is enough, and every message gets an answer. A pilot is a paid service on your own server; a function that is not built yet is listed as planned, never as available.
