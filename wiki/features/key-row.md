# The key row

The row of keys under the terminal for what a phone keyboard lacks. Back to the [README](../../README.md). Related: [Workspaces and tabs](./workspaces.md), [Concepts](../concepts.md).

## The keys

Left to right, the default row has `/`, Ctrl+C, Esc, a new line that does not send, Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Tab, Enter, Fn, the up and down arrows, and dictation and its language (only where the browser can dictate). The keyboard key and **A−** and **A+** are under **Add a key** in Settings; pinch the terminal to change the text size. Tap Ctrl or Alt first, then the key to combine it with.

Keys that do not fit the screen, and keys you took off the row, are under **More keys**. **Close** closes that list.

## Change the row

Open Settings, then **Appearance & notifications**, and find Key row. Choose which row you edit, Shell or tmux. Pick a **Preset**, or move keys earlier or later, remove them, or use **Add a key**. **Reset to today's row** puts the default back. The row is kept on this device.

## On a desktop

**On a desktop** decides what a computer shows. **Auto (by pointer)** shows one hint line instead of the row when a mouse or trackpad is the main pointer; **Full row** keeps the keys, which suits a touch laptop; **Compact** is the short bar. The short bar has **Paste**, **Copy**, **Select** and the text size keys next to the hint.
